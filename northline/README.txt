NORTHLINE WEBSITE
=================
Open index.html in a browser.

Files:
- index.html — semantic page structure
- style.css — responsive premium styling
- script.js — menu, scroll reveals, counters, cursor and motion

Notes:
- Images are loaded from Unsplash URLs, so an internet connection is needed for the imagery.
- Google Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
- The site is a fictional/demo Northline concept.
- Replace image URLs, email, social links and copy with your production content when needed.
